# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A practical guide to how OctoAcme initiates, plans, executes, releases, and improves projects. Use these documents as a shared reference for repeatable delivery, clear ownership, stakeholder alignment, and continuous learning.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme starts by validating a business need, defining measurable outcomes, identifying stakeholders, and deciding whether to proceed. Approved work is planned into prioritized, estimable backlog items with acceptance criteria, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified risks and dependencies. During execution, teams track work on a project board, collaborate through small reviewed pull requests, test changes, monitor delivery signals, and escalate blockers. Releases follow pre-release checks, deployment verification, stakeholder communication, and rollback planning. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, teams capture lessons and track improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, and communication; Product Managers own outcomes and prioritization; developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute according to their responsibilities.
