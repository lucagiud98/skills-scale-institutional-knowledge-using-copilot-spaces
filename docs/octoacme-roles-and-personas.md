# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers or Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers translate customer and user needs into clear product experiences, flows, and validation plans. They help the team design usable, accessible solutions that align with product goals and delivery constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Convert product goals into user flows, wireframes, and interface design
- Partner with Product Managers to validate assumptions and prioritize experience improvements
- Work with Developers and QA to ensure designs are feasible and tested against acceptance criteria
- Document design decisions and edge cases that affect usability or accessibility

### Goals
- Improve user experience and adoption
- Reduce ambiguity in product decisions
- Align product experience with customer needs and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and roadmap reviews
- Design critiques and usability feedback sessions
- Handoff to Developers and QA through specs, mockups, and review notes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to prioritize user problems and validate solutions
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure implementation matches the intended experience
- Supports QA by clarifying expected behavior, edge cases, and usability acceptance criteria
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design dependencies, risks, or delays that could affect milestones

---

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define the measurement plan that connects product work to business outcomes. They help teams understand whether value is being delivered and where to improve based on evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation, dashboards, and success metrics with Product Managers
- Partner with Developers on event tracking and data quality requirements
- Validate reporting accuracy and interpret trends for stakeholders
- Highlight risks, gaps, or anomalies that affect decisions or reporting
- Recommend product or process adjustments based on observed customer or operational data

### Goals
- Measure impact with trustworthy data
- Improve decision-making using evidence rather than assumptions
- Support continuous learning and iteration

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and roadmap check-ins
- Data quality and instrumentation discussions with engineering
- Stakeholder updates on metrics, trends, and recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on the desired outcomes and success metrics
- Partners with Developers to ensure tracking is implemented correctly and consistently
- Provides Project Managers with trend updates that may influence schedule, release readiness, or stakeholder messaging
- Shares insight with QA and support teams when data indicates feature quality or adoption issues

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads protect the product, customers, and business by identifying and mitigating compliance, data, and security risks throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review products, processes, and dependencies for security and privacy risks
- Define controls, review requirements, and recommend mitigations
- Support threat modeling, access reviews, privacy checks, and data handling decisions
- Partner with Developers and QA to verify fixes, controls, and release readiness
- Coordinate escalation and incident response communication when critical risks arise

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Protect customer trust and sensitive data
- Ensure delivery decisions account for risk and control requirements

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews
- Risk assessments and remediation planning
- Escalations during incidents, release gating, or compliance reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review design, dependencies, and implementation choices
- Supports QA by helping define validation for security, privacy, and data-handling requirements
- Advises Project Managers on risk severity, mitigation plans, and when decisions require escalation
- Coordinates with Product Managers when a trade-off affects scope, timeline, or customer trust

---

## Operations, SRE, or Release Owners

### Role Summary
Operations, SRE, and Release Owners help teams deliver reliable services and smooth deployments. They focus on deployment readiness, system health, rollback planning, and sustainable service operations.

### Responsibilities
- Define release windows, release readiness criteria, and rollback plans
- Monitor reliability, observability, and production health indicators
- Partner with Developers and QA on deployment validation and service readiness checks
- Escalate operational risks and coordinate mitigation before or after release
- Support post-release monitoring and issue triage when a change affects production stability

### Goals
- Reduce operational risk during deployment and release
- Improve service availability and resilience
- Support predictable delivery without disruption to customers

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment reviews
- Incident or outage coordination
- Production readiness and post-release health check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Project Managers sequence release milestones and communicate dependencies
- Works with Developers and QA to confirm production readiness and rollback criteria
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer impact and release communications
- Supports stakeholders with deployment status, known issues, and mitigation steps

---

## Customer Support or Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success representatives connect product delivery to customer experience and adoption. They help the team understand customer friction, support needs, and post-release feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Bring customer pain points, support patterns, and known issues into planning discussions
- Help define support readiness, documentation, and user guidance for new features
- Share real-world customer feedback that informs product improvements and releases
- Coordinate with Product Managers on customer impact and expected outcomes
- Contribute to launch readiness, training, and stakeholder communication when needed

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce avoidable support load and service disruption
- Make sure the product experience is operationally supportable

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and support trend analysis
- Release readiness and launch planning sessions
- Follow-up after release to assess adoption, issues, and support needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to interpret customer needs and business impact
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align communication and release timing
- Shares insights with Developers and QA to identify defects, usability issues, and support gaps
- Helps stakeholders understand post-release success, adoption trends, and customer sentiment

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- For smaller teams, one person may cover multiple roles, but responsibilities and decision ownership should still be clearly documented.
